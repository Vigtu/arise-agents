# Haste Potion

> *"Time slows around the shadows. Their movements become a blur."*

**Rank:** A (Rare Consumable)
**Type:** Session Buff
**Origin:** Temporal alchemy — distilled from the Monarch's control over time
**Effect:** Accelerated Temporal Field — 2.5x output speed

---

## Lore

In the depths of the Demon Castle, time flows differently. The Monarch learned to bend it — not to see the future, but to move faster than any enemy could react.

The Haste Potion distills this power into a consumable form. When consumed, a temporal field envelops the entire Shadow Army. Their movements blur, their strikes land before the enemy can blink.

But temporal manipulation has a cost. Mana drains **6x faster** while the field holds. The wise Monarch uses it for swift raids and rapid assaults — not prolonged wars of attrition.

## Effect Details

When consumed, the Monarch activates **Accelerated Temporal Field**:

### Cooldown Mechanic

When mana runs dry:
1. Temporal field **weakens** — fallback to standard speed
2. Shadows continue at normal speed
3. When mana recharges, the field **reactivates automatically**

### Best For

- Rapid iteration on code changes
- Live debugging sessions
- Quick shadow scouts and reviews

### Not Recommended For

- Long autonomous refactoring (Tusk's heavy lifting)
- Cost-sensitive workloads
